# Survey summary: junior developer job search

- Date: 28.09.2026
- Run by: Daniel Ivanenko (Product Lead)
- Format: anonymous Google Form, "JobBridge — Junior developer job search (2 min, anonymous)"
- Responses: 9
- Raw responses stay in the team's private Google Drive. They contain contact handles, so they are not copied here. Respondents are numbered R1–R9 in the order they answered.

All numbers below are **self-reported** by the respondents. Nothing was measured by us.

**Out of the last 10 applications, how many could they attach objective proof of a specific skill to**

R1–R9: 1, 10, 3, 7, 8, 7, 5, 8, 5. Median 7, mean 6.0. Actively applying only (R2, R3, R6, R8): 10, 3, 7, 8.

**How much more likely to apply / feel confident with a free, objective, shareable skill report** (1 = not at all, 5 = much more)

R1–R9: 4, 3, 4, 5, 5, 2, 3, 4, 5. Mean 3.9. Six of nine answered 4 or 5.

**Willing to do a 15-minute follow-up chat and be quoted anonymously:** 3 of 9.

## [our note] What this tells us

- 7 of 9 stop before any interview: rejected after the CV screen (5) or no reply (2).
- 6 of 9 get a generic rejection with no reason (5) or no reply at all (1).
- 6 of 9 need a few days or longer to get an objective skill assessment, or cannot get one.
- The "proof in last 10 applications" answers (median 7) do not support the idea that juniors have no proof. We think many counted their GitHub or personal projects as proof, so the question was probably read in different ways. We need to ask about this in the follow-up chats.
- Small sample, and four respondents are not applying yet.

## Open questions

- What counts as "objective proof" for them, and for an employer?
- Would an employer actually open a JobBridge report?
